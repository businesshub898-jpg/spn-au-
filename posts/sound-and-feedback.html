Travel Light, Feel Free

A few smart packing choices can make getting around easier and more enjoyable.

Ruby & fortune travels • Travel tips

A few smart packing choices can make getting around easier and more enjoyable.

Build a simple packing list

Start with the essentials, then add clothes that work together and suit the weather at your destination.

Keep important items close

Store travel documents, medication, chargers, and a change of clothes where they are easy to reach.

Make room for the journey

Leave a little space for local finds and check baggage rules before heading to the airport.
