Ruby & fortune travels • Travel journal

Some of the best travel memories begin with a recommendation from someone who knows the place.

Ask curious questions

Chat with a shopkeeper, guide, or host about the places they return to and the dishes they love.

Explore beyond the main square

Wander into quieter streets, neighborhood markets, and small cafés to find a different side of town.

Be a thoughtful guest

Learn a few local phrases, respect customs, and support independent businesses when you can.
