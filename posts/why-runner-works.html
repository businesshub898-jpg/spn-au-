Plan a Trip You’ll Remember

A little thoughtful planning can make room for more discovery along the way.

Ruby & fortune travels • Travel guide

A little thoughtful planning can make room for more discovery along the way.

Start with what you love

Choose the experiences that matter most, from mountain trails and local food to art, history, or quiet beaches.

Give your days a rhythm

Group nearby sights together, balance busy outings with downtime, and avoid trying to fit everything into one day.

Leave space for surprises

Keep an afternoon open for a neighborhood stroll, a market find, or a recommendation from someone local.
