Stay Safe on the Road

Simple precautions can help you travel with more confidence wherever you go.

Ruby & fortune travels • Travel tips

Simple precautions can help you travel with more confidence wherever you go.

Keep essentials secure

Carry only what you need for the day and keep important documents and valuables in a safe place.

Know your surroundings

Check local transport options and neighborhood guidance, especially when arriving late or traveling alone.

Share your plans

Let someone you trust know your itinerary and keep useful local contact details on hand.
