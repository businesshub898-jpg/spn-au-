About Ruby & fortune travels

We share travel inspiration and practical guidance to help make your next journey memorable.

Our Story: A Love of Exploring

Ruby & fortune travels grew from a love of discovering new places, sharing good stories, and meeting the people who give each destination its character.

We bring together destination inspiration, practical planning advice, and local highlights for trips of every pace—from a quick city break to a longer adventure.

Our mission is to help travelers feel prepared, stay open to discovery, and make lasting memories along the way.

Our Guiding Principles

These principles guide the travel ideas and advice we share with our community.

Traveler Focused

We aim to make our guides welcoming and useful for curious travelers with different interests and experience levels.

Accuracy & Insight

We value clear, practical information and encourage travelers to confirm details that may change before setting out.

Respect for Local Culture

We celebrate the traditions and communities that make each place special and encourage thoughtful ways to explore.

Community Focused

Travel stories are better when shared. We welcome tips, questions, and recommendations from fellow explorers.
